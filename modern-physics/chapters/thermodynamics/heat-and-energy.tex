\chapter{Heat and Energy}

Today we take it for granted that matter is composed of \textbf{atoms},
but proving so has not been simple or easy.

The earliest people to conceive the concept of the atom
where the ancient Greeks.
In fact,
the word "atom" originates from the Ancient Greek word for "uncuttable".
However, their reasons for believing in the atom
were more philosophical than scientific.

The scientific concept of atoms would only arise many years later.
